# ADR 0005: Ultraviolet visual identity

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-23

Use Ultraviolet as Updatarr's brand direction: `#A78BFA` for highlights and
marks on dark surfaces, and `#6D28D9` for accessible controls and focus on light
surfaces. The user selected it after reviewing the wider *arr color survey and
judging Seerr and Cleanuparr insufficiently common to outweigh the preference.

Status colors remain semantically separate from the brand. Updatarr's distinct
identity comes from its controlled-change transaction rail, update-specific
mark and operational layout as well as color. Secondary tokens remain subject
to representative light/dark prototype review.
